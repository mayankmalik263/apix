How the relay works

Read this once, then never again

One person starts each round. The other two cannot begin until they finish.

Every round has a STARTER — marked with a purple border — whose task is deliberately short, usually 15 to 40 minutes. Their output is what unblocks the other two. Then all three run in parallel for the rest of the round, and the baton passes to whoever starts the next one.

The baton order across the whole build is Ayush → Bharat → Bharat → Mayank → all three. It is not a rotation for fairness; it follows what actually blocks what.

Rule that matters more than the schedule: when you finish your task, say so out loud in the room. You are all in one place — use that. A finished task nobody knows about is the same as an unfinished one.

The five-minute Q&A — answers, not notes

QuestionWho answersThe answer
Which index formula?BharatJevons — geometric mean — for elementary aggregation across booking windows; Laspeyres-type fixed weights across routes. The same elementary form CPI construction uses.
Why median not mean?BharatFare distributions are right-skewed. On DEL–BOM, one route one date, we measured ₹6,090 to ₹24,056. The mean is ~₹8,900, a price almost nobody paid. The median describes the fare a typical buyer sees.
Where's the AI?MayankThere is none, deliberately. This measures, it doesn't predict. A model would make the number harder to audit, and a statistical office needs to defend every published figure.
The PS asks for CAPTCHA handling and IP rotation.MayankDeliberately absent. The same statement also requires robots.txt and ToS compliance — both cannot hold. We built a permission-tiered architecture instead, and we report the sources that refuse as a finding about market observability.
How much of this is real?MayankThe MoSPI overlay is real official data. The compliance checks are real, run today. The collection is real — 30 cells a day from a permitted source. The 91-day history behind it is simulated from a documented model, labelled SIMULATED in the database, and drawn differently on the chart.
Six routes out of 1,100 city pairs?BharatAnswer per route, never in aggregate. Five observations a day is ~150 a month against MoSPI's one national monthly figure. Adding a route is one line of config.
A festival surge and a broken parser both look like a spike.AyushThey arrive as different statuses. A surge is a run of OK rows and the median resists it. A broken parser is PARSE_FAIL — no fare, coverage drops, confidence downgrades, and Bronze is kept so the history can be re-parsed.
What if a portal redesigns its page?MayankRows go to PARSE_FAIL, coverage falls, the grade drops, and the series says so rather than quietly interpolating. Because Bronze is immutable, fixing the adapter recovers the history retroactively.
